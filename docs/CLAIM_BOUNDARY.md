# Claim Boundary

## Permitted claims

This manuscript may claim that:

1. Generative AI can help non-experts produce research-like artifacts such as papers, code, figures, repositories, QA notes, and submission metadata.
2. In the two author-supplied cases examined here, the artifacts were ultimately reclassified as claim-boundary or failure-boundary records rather than as validated technical or scientific results.
3. The battery case did not reach practical battery technology, BMS readiness, safe-charging claims, degradation-reduction claims, or real-cell validation.
4. The Hubble-tension case did not reach a cosmological solution, Lambda-CDM replacement, real-data likelihood, MCMC posterior, or evidence comparison.
5. Non-expert AI-assisted research should include explicit grounding and claim-boundary gates before public technical or scientific claims are made.

## Prohibited claims

This manuscript must not be presented as claiming that:

1. AI-assisted research is useless or impossible.
2. Expert-led AI-assisted science has been evaluated or discredited.
3. The author has created a practical battery technology, BMS method, safe-charging protocol, degradation-reduction method, lithium-plating avoidance method, or EV/automotive safety result.
4. The author has created a Hubble-tension solution, a Lambda-CDM replacement, a validated cosmological model, an observational likelihood result, a posterior distribution, or an evidence comparison.
5. The two cases statistically prove a general rule about all AI-assisted research.
6. The internal case archives are independent external evidence of domain validity.

## Recommended public framing

Use this framing:

> This is an AI-assisted reflexive case study about non-expert AI-assisted research artifacts and claim-boundary control. It is not a battery-technology paper and not a cosmological result.

Avoid this framing:

> This paper proves that AI cannot do research.

Also avoid this framing:

> This paper introduces a new battery or cosmology theory.
