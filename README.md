This repository archives a claim-bounded, AI-assisted reflexive case study on **artifact inflation**, **epistemic grounding failure**, and **claim-boundary control** in non-expert AI-assisted research.

The preserved manuscript is the v0.1.2 ai.vixra-oriented final manuscript. This v0.1.3 release does **not** introduce new case claims. It reorganizes the public repository, strengthens orientation material, adds a GitHub Pages landing structure, refreshes metadata, adds licensing/context files, and regenerates the manifest.

## What this is

This is a methodological and reflexive case study of two author-supplied AI-assisted project histories:

1. a lithium-ion battery-model audit that was reduced from technology-oriented ambition to a bounded residual-drift / expert-review record; and
2. an A10-derived Hubble-tension scaffold audit that was reduced from cosmological ambition to a failure-boundary archive after no physical E(z)/H(z) bridge, likelihood route, MCMC posterior, or evidence comparison was established.

The central lesson is not that AI-assisted research is useless. The narrower claim is that non-expert AI-assisted research can rapidly produce complete-looking artifacts—papers, repositories, figures, manifests, submission metadata, QA notes—without thereby establishing domain validity.

## Claim boundary

This repository does **not** claim:

- a practical battery technology;
- a battery-management-system method;
- a safe-charging protocol;
- degradation-reduction, lithium-plating avoidance, or EV/automotive safety results;
- a Hubble-tension solution;
- a Lambda-CDM replacement;
- a validated cosmological model;
- a real-data likelihood, MCMC posterior, or Bayesian evidence comparison;
- a statistical proof about all AI-assisted research; or
- an evaluation of expert-led AI-assisted science.

Recommended public framing:

> This is an AI-assisted reflexive case study about non-expert AI-assisted research artifacts and claim-boundary control. It is not a battery-technology paper and not a cosmological result.
