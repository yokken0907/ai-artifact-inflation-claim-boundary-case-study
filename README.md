## Scope

This manuscript is a reflexive case study of two AI-assisted non-expert research attempts:

1. a lithium-ion battery-model audit, and
2. an A10-derived Hubble-tension scaffold audit.

It does **not** claim a practical battery technology, BMS method, safe-charging protocol, degradation-reduction result, cosmological solution, Lambda-CDM replacement, observational likelihood result, MCMC posterior, or evidence comparison.

The central claim is methodological: AI can rapidly inflate the visible surface of research artifacts, but artifact completeness does not by itself establish domain validity. Non-expert AI-assisted research therefore requires explicit domain-grounding, prior-art, external-data, implementation-or-experiment, expert-review-worthiness, and public-claim-boundary gates.
